'split-view-extension': minor
---

With no split view open — as after the extension is updated, which closes their tabs — the toolbar button opens every saved split view in a tab of its own, the one looked at last in front, instead of only that one. While any split view is open it goes back to the one looked at last, as before. A split view that loads in a background tab no longer takes over as the one the button goes back to until it is shown.
