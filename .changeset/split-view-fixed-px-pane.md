A split can hold one pane at a fixed width or height in pixels, the other pane taking the rest (`r737fpp` in a `layout=` spec), and dragging its divider keeps it in pixels. A pull request now opens with the diff held at the narrowest width that keeps GitHub's file tree (737px beside the conversation alone, 554px at 75% in three columns); beside the diff alone the conversation takes the rest of the page, and in three columns it is held at 577px, the widest its description gets with the reviewers and labels below it, the issue or the session taking the rest.
