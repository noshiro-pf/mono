Add `Tag`, `TagInput`, `RangeSlider` and `BpText` (Blueprint's `Text`), in Blueprint's markup. `TagInput` adds what is typed, pasted or left on blur, and moves through and removes its tags by the keys; `RangeSlider` is dragged, pressed on its track and keyed, each handle stopping at the other. `MenuItem` now cuts its text short through `BpText`.
