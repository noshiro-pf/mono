---
'split-view-extension': minor
---

Adds "Open in a split to the right" and "Open in a split below" to the context menu of web pages and links, titled in Japanese when Chrome's UI is: in a split view they split the pane the menu was opened in, as its split buttons do, and open the link in the new pane; anywhere else they open the page beside the link, or beside an empty pane, in a new split view that replaces the tab.
